# battleship-app

# About
A streamlit game of battleship where the player battle the computer to guess and sink their ship! The player places a ship on a square and so does the computer on seperate boards. They each take turns 'shooting' squares on their boards and the first one to 'sink' the opponents ship wins

# Improvements
Improved the quality of UI in terms of the battleship squares as well as adding infinite turns and less squares to make the game more interesting to play
